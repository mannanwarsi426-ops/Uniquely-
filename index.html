About Us

Born out of a deep love for street-style tea, Chai Sutta is more than just a café—it's a community hub. We source the finest tea leaves and blend them with freshly ground spices to bring you that nostalgic, perfect cup every single time. Whether you need a morning kickstart or a relaxing evening vibe, we've got a freshly brewed cup waiting for you.
